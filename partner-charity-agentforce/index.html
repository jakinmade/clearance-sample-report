An AI check for every charity you move to Agentforce Nonprofit

Your migration projects already touch the data and processes the new agents will use. These checks let you sign off the AI side too, under your own brand.

  • Salesforce has renamed Nonprofit Cloud as Agentforce Nonprofit and added prebuilt agents for fundraising, programme management, volunteer coordination and donor support (Salesforce announcement).
  • Since December 2025, new Power of Us applicants get free Agentforce Nonprofit licences in place of NPSP, and NPSP charities are being advised to plan for migration (Cloud for Good).
  • The revised Charity Governance Code (November 2025) recommends an AI and technology policy, and trustees stay responsible for AI use whether or not the board knew about it (HCR Law; Lubbock Fine, June 2026).
  • The Fundraising Regulator's AI guidance expects human checks, labelling where needed and records of those checks (Guidance for using AI in fundraising, 8 December 2025).

Where it fits in a migration

  1. Before agents go liveAQE qualifies the planned agent or AI feature: go, go with conditions, or no-go, with a named owner.
  2. Board sign-offThe Trustee Check gives the board its own view, with a pre-filled AI policy.
  3. Once it's runningCLEARANCE checks the AI in use, including platform AI features, and shows where control can't be evidenced.
