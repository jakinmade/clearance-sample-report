AI assurance for councils and public bodies, under your brand

Qualify AI before it goes live, then check the AI already in use against the duties that already apply.

  • UK GDPR Articles 22A–22D set the safeguards for significant automated decisions, in force from 5 February 2026 (The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (Commencement) Regulations, SI 2026/82).
  • The Public Sector Equality Duty applies to AI-assisted decisions, including those an officer reviews (Equality Act 2010, s.149).
  • The Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard is mandatory for government departments and arm's-length bodies that deliver public or frontline services, and encouraged for councils (GOV.UK ATRS hub).
