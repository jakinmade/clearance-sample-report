How the findings are made

Fixed rules, not AI judgement

Every finding comes from a fixed rule library. The same answers always give the same result. No AI decides what is found.

Tied to your obligations

Each gap is linked to the obligations it touches, such as FCA Principles, SM&CR and UK GDPR, with a first step to close it.

A report you can stand behind

The full report is digitally sealed, so a board, auditor or insurer can check it has not been changed since it was issued.
