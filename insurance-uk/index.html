What was found

Each gap fires only when specific answers are present, and the report shows which ones. For each, the report states what it looks like when it goes wrong and the first thing to do about it.

SeverityGap and what it looks like in practiceFirst action
HIGHAI-assisted underwriting or claims decisioning delegated to a coverholder or DCA without managing agent oversightLloyd's Delegated Authorities team asks the managing agent whether any coverholder operating under its binders uses AI in claims decisioning. The managing agent cannot answer, it has never asked, and the binder does not require the coverholder to say.Amend the binder to require AI disclosure. Add an AI-use field to bordereaux templates. Extend the existing delegated authority audit programme to cover AI governance specifically. These are contractual and process controls, they do not require the managing agent to touch the coverholder's technology.
HIGHAI-driven renewal pricing model without evidence it satisfies the Equivalent New Business Price ruleThe FCA's GIPP multi-firm review asks the firm to produce the evidence its senior manager relied on to attest ENBP compliance for its AI-driven pricing model. The firm has a pricing policy document. It does not have model-specific evidence, and the attestor signed anyway.Build the ENBP evidence pack for the model's actual output. Test for tenure-proxy variables in the feature set. Brief the attestor on the model specifically before the next annual sign-off. These are documentation and testing controls, they do not require rebuilding the pricing model itself.
HIGHAI underwriting or pricing model uses protected-characteristic proxies without an actuarial justification the firm can evidenceA journalist asks the firm whether its underwriting model prices differently by postcode in a way that reproduces a racial pattern. The firm has never tested for this itself and has no actuarial file to point to, for age or disability it might have had a defence; for this, it has none.Test the model's feature set against each protected characteristic separately, since the legal position differs by characteristic. Build the actuarial justification file for age and disability features that pass. Remove or retire any feature correlating with sex or race. Repeat testing after every retrain.
HIGHAI-driven underwriting or pricing decision affects vulnerable customers without FG21/1-aligned safeguardsThe FCA's independent review of travel underwriting for pre-existing mental health conditions lands on the firm's desk. The firm has never looked at its own AI model's decline pattern against that specific criterion, the regulator's review is the first time anyone has.Build vulnerability flagging into the underwriting workflow. Run the mental-health/travel-insurance and social-renter/contents-insurance comparisons internally now. Train staff on override authority. These are review and process controls, not a rebuild of the underwriting model.
HIGHAudit voidA client disputes a decision and asks to see the record. The decision happened. The record of it did not.Start logging every decision this system makes today: date, input, output, rule applied, in a write-once store. This is the foundation. Every other governance control depends on it existing.
MEDIUMProvenance weaknessA regulator or auditor asks where the data came from that drove a specific decision. Tracing it back takes days, not minutes, and for some inputs there is no trail at all.Create a register of every data source feeding this system. Assign an owner and a validation status to each.
